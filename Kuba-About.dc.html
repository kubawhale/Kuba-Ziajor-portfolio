About

Designer, and the person who solders it together after.

I work between three things that keep turning out to be the same thing: interfaces, instruments, and the tools that sit in between. Screens teach me how people expect to be understood; hardware keeps me honest about what actually gets built.

Based in Rzeszów, Poland. Most weeks that means wireframes and design-system work by day, and a bench with a Daisy Seed on it in the evening.
